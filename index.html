Questions you may have

Make your next decision with clarity.

The key details, without unnecessary jargon.

Can I study while working full-time?

The Amity Online MBA is presented as an online programme with live and recorded learning. The reference page suggests planning around 12–15 hours per week; your actual schedule may vary by programme and study pace.

How long does the Online MBA take?

The reference page describes the MBA as a two-year programme across four semesters. Confirm the current structure and credit requirements before applying.

Will an MBA guarantee a promotion or higher salary?

No programme can guarantee a promotion, salary increase or job. Outcomes depend on factors including experience, skills, performance, employer requirements and market conditions.

How is the 45% merit-based scholarship applied?

The offer shown on this campaign is “up to 45%” for eligible Online MBA applicants. The final amount, eligibility criteria, payment method and whether it can be combined with other offers must be confirmed with admissions.

Can I compare fees and payment options before applying?

Yes. This page lists the fee schedule supplied for the programmes. The one-time payment option with an 8% discount and annual payment option with a 5% discount are separate; ask admissions to confirm the current amount applicable to your application.

Does the hiring-partner network guarantee a job?

No. A recruitment or hiring-partner network and career resources do not guarantee placement, an interview or an employment outcome.
